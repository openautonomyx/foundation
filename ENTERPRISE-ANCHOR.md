# Enterprise Anchor

OpenAutonomyX is anchored on enterprise operating reality.

The foundation exists to support institutions that must operate reliably across people, teams, systems, vendors, jurisdictions, controls, risks, audits, incidents, budgets, and changing technology landscapes.

## Enterprise Assumption

OpenAutonomyX systems are designed for environments where:

- accountability matters
- auditability matters
- security matters
- privacy matters
- compliance matters
- reliability matters
- procurement matters
- integration matters
- data governance matters
- identity and access matter
- operational continuity matters
- measurable value matters

Enterprise use is not an afterthought. It is the baseline.

## Enterprise Stakeholders

OpenAutonomyX must serve multiple institutional stakeholders, including:

- executive sponsors
- business owners
- product owners
- IT administrators
- enterprise architects
- security teams
- risk and compliance teams
- legal and privacy teams
- finance and procurement teams
- data governance teams
- operations teams
- auditors

- affected stakeholders
