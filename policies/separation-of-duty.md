# Separation of Duty Policy

## Purpose

Separation of duty ensures that consequential changes are not defined, approved, deployed, and audited by the same actor without independent review.

## Policy Statement

No single human, agent, service, or organization should unilaterally control all stages of a high-impact action.

## Applies To

This policy applies to:

- production deployments
- policy changes
- access changes
- identity issuance
- trust certification
- exception approval
- financial authorization
- safety override
- incident closure
- publication approval

## Required Separation

At minimum, consequential work should separate:

- requester
- implementer
- approver
- auditor

## Exceptions

Exceptions must record:

- reason
- scope
- approver
- duration
- compensating controls
- review date

## Principle

Autonomy scales safely when power is distributed, reviewed, and accountable.
