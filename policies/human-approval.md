# Human Approval Policy

## Purpose

Human approval ensures that consequential actions remain accountable, reviewable, and aligned with human intent, institutional responsibility, and applicable obligations.

## Policy Statement

Humans must retain meaningful control over consequential decisions, irreversible actions, high-risk automation, policy exceptions, and actions that materially affect people, institutions, assets, trust, privacy, security, compliance, or public communication.

## Requires Approval

Human approval is required for:

- production deployment with material impact
- identity issuance or revocation
- privileged access changes
- trust certification or revocation
- policy creation or material policy change
- exception approval
- safety override
- privacy-impacting data processing
- publication of official material
- financial authorization above approved limits
- irreversible deletion or destructive action
- autonomous execution above defined risk threshold

## Approval Requirements

An approval request should include:

- requester
- actor
- target
- purpose
- expected outcome
- risk level
- policy context
- evidence
- rollback path
- expiry or review date where applicable

## Delegation

Approval authority may be delegated only when delegation is explicit, scoped, time-bounded, auditable, and revocable.

## Emergency Approval

Emergency approval may be used when delay creates material risk.

Emergency approvals must be reviewed after the fact and must record:

- emergency reason
- approving authority
- actions taken
- evidence
- compensating controls
- follow-up review

## Appeals and Overrides

Affected humans should have a path to appeal consequential automated decisions where applicable.

Overrides must record reason, authority, scope, duration, and evidence.

## Principle

Humans approve; systems reconcile. Autonomy must increase human agency, not erase accountability.
