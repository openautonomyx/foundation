# Privacy and Consent Policy

## Purpose

Privacy and consent ensure that personal, sensitive, confidential, and regulated data is collected, processed, retained, shared, and deleted responsibly.

## Policy Statement

OpenAutonomyX systems must respect privacy, purpose limitation, consent, data minimization, security, retention, deletion, and applicable law.

## Core Requirements

Systems handling personal or sensitive data should define:

- purpose of collection
- lawful or approved basis for processing
- consent requirement where applicable
- data categories
- data subjects
- processing activities
- retention period
- deletion process
- sharing rules
- access controls
- audit requirements

## Consent Lifecycle

Consent should be:

- explicit where required
- purpose-specific
- time-aware
- revocable
- auditable
- understandable
- separate from unrelated consent where practical

## Data Minimization

Systems should collect and retain only the data necessary for the stated purpose.

## Withdrawal and Deletion

Where applicable, individuals must have a path to withdraw consent, request deletion, or request correction.

## AI and Agent Processing

AI and agent systems must not use personal or sensitive data beyond the approved purpose, policy scope, access boundary, or consent context.

## Principle

Autonomy must not come at the cost of privacy, consent, or human dignity.
