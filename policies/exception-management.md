# Exception Management Policy

## Purpose

Exception management ensures that temporary deviations from policy are explicit, justified, approved, time-bounded, reviewed, and auditable.

## Policy Statement

Policies may be overridden only through a controlled exception process. Exceptions must not become hidden defaults or permanent bypasses.

## Applies To

This policy applies to exceptions involving:

- access control
- identity lifecycle
- trust requirements
- security controls
- privacy and consent controls
- reliability expectations
- separation of duty
- human approval
- publication approval
- compliance obligations
- production operations

## Exception Record

Every exception must record:

- requester
- approver
- policy being excepted
- reason
- scope
- affected entities or artifacts
- risk assessment
- compensating controls
- start date
- expiry date
- review date
- revocation conditions
- evidence

## Approval

Exceptions must be approved by an authorized human or governance body with sufficient authority for the risk level.

High-risk exceptions require additional review and separation of duty.

## Expiry

Exceptions must be time-bounded by default.

Expired exceptions must be revoked, renewed through review, or converted into an approved policy change.

## Emergency Exceptions

Emergency exceptions may be granted when delay would create material harm.

Emergency exceptions must be reviewed after the fact and must include evidence, justification, and corrective action.

## Principle

A well-governed system can bend under justified conditions without silently breaking its rules.
