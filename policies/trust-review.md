# Trust Review Policy

## Purpose

Trust review ensures that trust assertions are issued, renewed, suspended, revoked, and restored through evidence-based governance.

## Policy Statement

Trust must not be permanent, implicit, or unverifiable. Trust must be based on identity, evidence, behavior, evaluation, accountability, and review.

## Applies To

This policy applies to trust assertions about:

- humans where lawful and appropriate
- agents
- organizations
- teams
- services
- applications
- devices
- tools
- datasets
- models
- artifacts
- workflows
- publishers
- integrations

## Trust Issuance

A trust assertion should define:

- subject
- issuer
- claim
- domain
- evidence
- confidence
- score where applicable
- validity period
- review date
- revocation conditions

## Trust Renewal

Trust must be periodically reviewed based on:

- fresh evidence
- evaluation results
- incident history
- audit findings
- policy compliance
- operational behavior
- security posture
- owner review

## Suspension and Revocation

Trust may be suspended or revoked when:

- evidence expires
- verification fails
- policy is violated
- reliability degrades
- security risk appears
- incident history changes
- ownership becomes unclear
- audit finding remains unresolved

## Restoration

Restoration requires evidence that the cause of suspension or revocation has been addressed.

## Principle

Trust is governed reliance, not blind belief.
