# Enterprise Decision Model

The OpenAutonomyX enterprise decision model specializes the foundation decision model for institutional decision-making across strategy, investment, architecture, risk, security, privacy, compliance, operations, products, and agent delegation.

## Purpose

Enterprises are decision systems. OpenAutonomyX improves decision quality, speed, traceability, accountability, and outcomes by making decisions explicit, evidence-backed, governed, reviewable, and connected to value.

## Decision Categories

Enterprise decision categories include:

- strategic decisions
- portfolio decisions
- investment decisions
- capability decisions
- architecture decisions
- security decisions
- privacy decisions
- compliance decisions
- risk acceptance decisions
- product decisions
- operational decisions
- incident decisions
- vendor decisions
- data governance decisions
- model selection decisions
- agent delegation decisions
- automation approval decisions
- exception approval decisions

- identifier
- title

- owner

- business context
- problem statement
- objectives

- constraints
- assumptions
- evidence
- risk impact
- control impact

- financial impact
- operational impact
- security impact
- privacy impact
- compliance impact
- recommendation
- confidence
- approval state
- decision status
- implementation path
- review date
- outcome
- value linkage

## Decision Lifecycle

```text
Identified
→ Framed
→ Analyzed
→ Reviewed
→ Approved or Rejected
→ Implemented
→ Measured
→ Reviewed
→ Superseded or Archived
```

## Evidence

Decision evidence may include:

- business case
- architecture review
- risk assessment
- control assessment
- security review
- privacy review
- compliance review
- financial analysis
- operational data
- user research
- benchmark results
- model evaluation
- incident history
- audit findings

## Human and Agent Roles

Agents may assist with research, option generation, evidence synthesis, risk analysis, policy checks, scenario modeling, and recommendation drafting.

Humans remain accountable for consequential decisions, risk acceptance, policy exceptions, and institutional commitments.

## Decision Quality

Decision quality should consider:

- clarity of problem
- completeness of options
- quality of evidence
- transparency of assumptions
- risk visibility
- policy compliance
- stakeholder alignment
- reversibility
- measured outcome

## Principle

A good enterprise decision is not only correct in the moment. It is explainable, reviewable, accountable, and connected to measurable outcomes.
