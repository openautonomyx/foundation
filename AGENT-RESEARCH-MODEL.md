# OpenAutonomyX Agent Research Model

The OpenAutonomyX agent research model defines how external research informs the foundation without being adopted uncritically.

## Purpose

The agent field moves quickly. Research (e.g. arXiv preprints) is a primary input, but it is a source of claims to be evaluated, not settled doctrine.

## Research Themes

The foundation tracks research across themes including:

- agent chains and multi-step / multi-agent pipelines
- data agents (agents over data, retrieval, and analytics)
- code agents (software engineering agents)
- planning, reasoning, and tool use
- memory and knowledge
- evaluation and safety
- agent interoperability protocols
- governance, alignment, and control

## Handling Research Inputs

Research artifacts are treated as knowledge assets with `claim` or `insight` status: they carry source, provenance, confidence, and validation state, and must be reviewed before influencing canonical doctrine or decisions.

## From Research to Doctrine

A research claim becomes doctrine only through evaluation and a decision record. Preprints are not authority.

## Integrity

Track provenance and version (e.g. arXiv id and revision); distinguish peer-reviewed from preprint; record confidence and known limitations.

## Research Principle

Treat every research output as a claim until validated. The foundation learns from research; it is not governed by it.
