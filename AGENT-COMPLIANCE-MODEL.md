# OpenAutonomyX Agent Compliance Model

The OpenAutonomyX agent compliance model defines how agents are aligned to laws, regulations, and recognized AI governance frameworks.

## Purpose

Agent autonomy must be demonstrably compliant. Compliance is continuous evidence that agents meet legal and framework obligations, not a one-time certification.

## Reference Frameworks

Agent compliance maps to recognized external frameworks, including:

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management systems)
- ISO/IEC 23894 (AI risk)
- EU AI Act obligations
- sector and vendor AI governance frameworks (e.g. cloud and platform AI compliance programs)

Mappings are versioned and carry confidence and evidence.

## Compliance Domains

- transparency and disclosure
- accountability and human oversight
- risk management and impact assessment
- data protection, privacy, and consent
- fairness and non-discrimination
- safety and robustness
- record-keeping and auditability
- incident reporting

## Continuous Compliance

Controls produce evidence inline; evaluation and audit verify it; drift and incidents trigger reassessment. Compliance state is a governed, reviewable attribute of each agent.

## Mapping to Controls

Foundation controls (human approval, separation of duty, traceability, auditability) are mapped to framework requirements so a single control can satisfy multiple obligations.

## Compliance Principle

Compliance is evidence, not assertion. An agent is compliant only as long as its evidence says so.
